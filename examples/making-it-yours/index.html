Making it yours

Two modules in, the site works. It also looks exactly like everyone else's.

This module changes that in three passes: the colours it wears, the way a reader moves through it, and the blocks that stop a reader who is about to make a mistake.

At a glance

Difficulty

Intermediate — you have built a site and written a few pages.

Time

About an hour, spread over three lessons.

Nothing here is required

A site with none of this turned on is a perfectly good site. Each lesson is one setting or one component — take the ones your readers need, leave the rest.

What you will have changed

LessonWhat it touches
Colourstheme/ — tokens, the dark scheme, one accent of yours
Navigationdocpensieve.config.mjs — menu, header, a page menu
CalloutsYour pages — blocks that carry a warning

The lessons

Colours
Give the site your palette — one token in the configuration, the dark scheme in a file of your own.
Updated 18 September 2026
Navigation
Forty pages a reader can move through — a menu that folds, links in the header, and a row of links inside a page.
Updated 18 September 2026
Callouts
Stop a reader who is about to make a mistake — six kinds of block, kinds of your own, and a mark to go with them.
Updated 18 September 2026
