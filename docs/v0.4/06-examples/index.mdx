# Examples

This section is not a manual. It is a **site built with DocPensieve**, the way a
team would build one: a training course in four modules, each a series of
lessons, with its authors, its dates, its tags and its components.

Everything here is written in Markdown and MDX, and reaches the reader without a
line of JavaScript.
