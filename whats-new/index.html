What's new in 0.4

This version is in preparation. Its betas go out under the npm tag beta, while the version installed by default stays the 0.3, documented in the latest pages of this site:

npx docpensieve@beta init my-site

Already there

The 0.4 has just opened. What follows is what it already brings, and the rest of its pages are still those of the 0.3. Each feature is announced here as it lands, with a link to the guide and to the reference — this page announces, it is never the only place something is written.

A menu that folds, and a panel of links in the header

foldedSidebar: true folds the categories of the menu, open on the branch the reader stands in. On a narrow screen the whole menu folds above the content, whatever that setting. And a headerLinks entry carrying columns opens a panel of links instead of leading anywhere — declared on its own, so a site can navigate from the header alone, or from both. See Navigation in the guide, and the configuration reference.

A menu of links, anywhere in a page

The Menu component places a row of links where a page needs them — a summary at the top of a landing page, the chapters of a guide. Entries can be grouped under a title, and the row folds behind a button on a narrow screen, without a script. See Menu.

For a 0.3 project

Nothing to change: a 0.3 configuration builds as it is. Migrate from latest to beta lists what changes on its own, what to check, and what is worth turning on.
